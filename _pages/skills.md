## Quantitative & Experimental Methods
Survey experiment design and factorial vignette studies, categorical data analysis, longitudinal and growth-curve modeling, latent variable / latent class analysis, network analysis (including graph convolutional networks), causal inference, multilevel modeling, and marginal-effects-based interpretation of linear and nonlinear models.

## Computational Methods
Machine learning (including doubly robust and gradient-boosting methods), natural-language and text-based measurement, and reproducible data workflows.

## Software & Programming
- **Stata** — including co-development of two published commands, [`meinequality`](/publication/mize-han-2025-sociological-science) and `totalme`, for summarizing the effects of nominal and ordinal variables across linear and nonlinear models
- **R** — data management, visualization, and statistical modeling
- Git/GitHub for reproducible research workflows

## Qualitative Methods
In-depth interviewing, thematic and grounded-theory coding, and mixed-methods research design.

## Teaching & Pedagogy
Course design and instruction in medical sociology, categorical data analysis, experimental methods, and longitudinal data analysis; methods pedagogy for the behavioral, health, and social sciences. See [Teaching](/teaching/) for details.
