<div class="intro__text" markdown="1">

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Population Research Institute at Penn State, where I work with Dr. Liying Luo on an NIH-funded R01 project examining educational disparities in Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. I received my dual-title Ph.D. in Sociology and Gerontology from Purdue University in 2025.

I am a medical sociologist and quantitative methodologist studying how social judgments and social structures shape health across the life course. My research examines how health behaviors and age become bases of stigma, and how gender, social relationships, and socioeconomic conditions contribute to inequalities in health and cognitive aging. I use survey experiments, longitudinal data, and computational and statistical methods.

My dissertation received the 2026 Best Dissertation Award from the American Sociological Association's Sociology of Mental Health Section and a Distinguished Dissertation Award from Purdue University's College of Liberal Arts.
