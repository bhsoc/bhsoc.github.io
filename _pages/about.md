Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the [Population Research Institute](https://popcenter.psu.edu/) at The Pennsylvania State University, working with Dr. Liying Luo on an NIH-funded R01 project (R01AG078518) examining educational disparities in Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. I completed my dual-title Ph.D. in [Sociology](https://www.cla.purdue.edu/academic/sociology/index.html) and [Gerontology](https://www.purdue.edu/aging/dualtitle/) at [Purdue University](https://www.purdue.edu/) in 2025, where I was advised by [Dr. Trenton Mize](https://www.trentonmize.com) and [Dr. Shawn Bauldry](https://sites.google.com/site/sgbauldry/).

I am a medical sociologist and quantitative methodologist studying how social structures and social judgments produce health inequalities across the life course. My research examines how health behaviors and age become bases of stigma, and how gender, education, race and ethnicity, social relationships, and environments shape health practices and cognitive and mental health across social groups. Using survey experiments, longitudinal data analysis, and network and computational methods, I identify the mechanisms through which social inequalities are produced, accumulated, and embodied across the life course. My current work develops a new agenda on ageism, social connectedness, and cognitive aging, including a National Institute on Aging K99/R00 proposal.

Alongside this substantive work, I develop methodological tools for applied researchers. With [Trenton Mize](https://www.trentonmize.com), I co-developed the Stata commands `meinequality` and `totalme` for summarizing the effects of categorical and ordinal variables in linear and nonlinear models.

My dissertation received the American Sociological Association Sociology of Mental Health Section's Best Dissertation Award and Purdue's Distinguished Dissertation Award.

Feel free to reach out at [bhan@psu.edu](mailto:bhan@psu.edu). I welcome opportunities for collaboration, discussion, and connection.
